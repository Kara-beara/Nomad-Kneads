Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information — including your rights around SMS communications.

Last updated: June 1, 2025
01 — Overview

Who We Are

Nomad Kneads ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile massage and wellness service based in Atlanta, Georgia, operating at nomadkneads.com and reachable at +1 (770) 802-1826.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described here.

02 — Data Collection

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (when you book a session or contact us)
  • Appointment details, service preferences, and health intake information
  • Billing and payment information (processed securely through Square)
  • Communications you send us via email, phone, or our website contact forms
  • Mobile phone number and SMS opt-in consent (when you choose to receive text messages)

We may also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs, for analytics and security purposes.

03 — SMS & Text Messaging

SMS Communications & Text Messaging Program

Nomad Kneads operates an SMS text messaging program to communicate appointment confirmations, reminders, and occasional promotional offers. This section describes how that program works and your rights as a participant.

📱 How You Opt In

You provide express written consent to receive SMS messages from Nomad Kneads by submitting your mobile phone number through the opt-in form on our website at nomadkneads.com. At the point of collection, a clear opt-in disclosure is displayed explaining the nature and frequency of messages.

Consent is not a condition of purchase. You are not required to opt in to receive SMS messages in order to book a massage session or use any of our services.

📨 Types of Messages We Send

  • Appointment confirmations and booking receipts
  • Appointment reminders (typically 24–48 hours before your session)
  • Rescheduling or cancellation notifications
  • Occasional promotional messages about new services, specials, or offers

Message frequency varies based on your appointment activity and preferences.

🛑 How to Opt Out

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any text message you receive from us. After texting STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.

You may also opt out by contacting us directly at hello@nomadkneads.com or by calling +1 (770) 802-1826.

❓ Help & Support

Reply HELP to any SMS message you receive from us to get assistance. You may also contact us at hello@nomadkneads.com or +1 (770) 802-1826.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard messaging and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply to messages you send and receive. Nomad Kneads is not responsible for charges from your mobile carrier.

No Sharing of SMS Opt-In Data: We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your mobile opt-in data will not be shared with any affiliate, partner, or other organization for marketing use. The only exception is service providers who assist us in operating our SMS program (such as our SMS platform provider), and those providers are contractually required to keep your information confidential and use it only to deliver messages on our behalf.

04 — Use of Data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and manage your massage appointments
  • Send appointment reminders, confirmations, and follow-ups
  • Send SMS messages you have consented to receive
  • Process payments and issue receipts
  • Respond to your questions, requests, and feedback
  • Improve our services and website experience
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

05 — Sharing

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We share data with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, such as Square (payment processing), our booking platform, and our SMS messaging provider. These providers are contractually bound to use your data only to provide services to us.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Protection of Rights: We may disclose information to protect the safety, rights, or property of Nomad Kneads, our clients, or the public.

We will never sell your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information to any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

06 — Security

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Payment information is processed through Square's PCI-compliant systems and is never stored directly on our servers.

While we work hard to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us promptly if you believe your information has been compromised.

07 — Your Rights

Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Opt out of promotional email communications by using the unsubscribe link in any email

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in the Contact section below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time frame.

08 — Cookies

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect some features of our website.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information or to track you across third-party websites for advertising purposes.

09 — Children

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

10 — Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
