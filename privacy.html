Privacy Policy

How Nomad Kneads collects, uses, and protects your personal information — including your rights regarding SMS text message communications.

Last updated: May 2025
SMS / Text Messaging — Your Rights

Text Message Consent & Opt-Out Information

  • Consent is never required to purchase. Opting in to SMS messages is never a condition of booking any service with Nomad Kneads.
  • What you'll receive. If you opt in, message types include: appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and occasional promotions or wellness tips.
  • How to opt out. Reply STOP to any text message at any time. You'll receive one final confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
  • Need help? Reply HELP to any message or email us at hello@nomadkneads.com.
  • Message & data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Message frequency varies.
  • We never share your number. Mobile opt-in data and consent information is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.
Section 01

Who We Are

Nomad Kneads is a licensed mobile massage therapy business serving the greater Atlanta, Georgia area. We provide in-home, in-office, and event massage therapy services.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website at nomadkneads.com, through our online booking system (powered by Square), through our SMS opt-in form, and through any direct communications you initiate with us.

By using our website or submitting your information to us, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide. This includes information submitted when you book a session, complete a health intake form, opt in to SMS communications, or contact us directly.

  • Contact information — your name, email address, phone number, and service location
  • Health history — medical conditions, medications, and related details provided on your intake form to ensure safe and effective treatment
  • Booking details — appointment dates and times, services selected, and payment records processed securely through Square
  • SMS consent data — your mobile phone number and opt-in status when you choose to receive text communications
  • Communications — messages you send us through email, text, or our website contact form

We do not collect any information automatically beyond standard server logs, and we do not use tracking pixels, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics that share your data.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate our business and serve you. We use it to:

  • Book, confirm, manage, and send reminders for your appointments
  • Deliver safe, personalized massage therapy based on your health history
  • Send appointment confirmations and reminders via email or SMS (if opted in)
  • Send occasional promotional messages about services or specials — only to clients who have explicitly opted in to SMS communications
  • Process payments securely through our Square booking platform
  • Respond to inquiries, questions, and service requests
  • Comply with applicable legal and professional licensing obligations

We do not use your personal information for any purpose beyond what is described above without first obtaining your explicit consent.

Section 04

SMS Text Messaging — Full Disclosure

Nomad Kneads operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with clients who have explicitly opted in. The following disclosures apply to all SMS communications:

  • Program description: Clients who opt in will receive text messages from Nomad Kneads including appointment confirmations, reminders, and occasional promotional messages about services and wellness tips.
  • Opt-in method: Consent is collected through a checkbox form on our website at nomadkneads.com. The checkbox is unchecked by default and must be actively selected by the user.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase: You are not required to opt in to receive SMS messages in order to book or receive any service.
  • Message frequency: Varies based on appointment activity. Typically 1–4 messages per month.
  • Message & data rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier and plan.
  • How to opt out: Reply STOP at any time. You will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts will be sent.
  • How to get help: Reply HELP or contact hello@nomadkneads.com.
  • No third-party sharing: Mobile opt-in information, phone numbers, and consent data are never sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any third party or affiliate for marketing or any other purpose.
Section 05

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Square (payment processor) — your booking and payment data is processed through Square's secure platform, subject to Square's own privacy policy
  • Legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business protection — to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nomad Kneads or its clients, where permitted by law

Under no circumstances is your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data shared with, sold to, or used by any third party for marketing purposes.

Section 06

Data Retention & Security

We retain client records, including health intake forms and booking history, for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with professional and legal obligations for licensed massage therapists in the state of Georgia.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Payment information is processed and stored securely by Square and is never stored on our own systems.

To request deletion of your personal data, please contact us at the address below.

Section 07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Withdraw consent for any other communications by contacting us directly

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Section 08

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and older, or minors accompanied by a parent or guardian who provides consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website or services after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify active clients.
